An evaluation starts with a real workflow and the requirements around it. Use the intended Higala scope to prepare questions for your team.

## For an institution

Choose one account or lending workflow. Record the users, approvals, data, reports, and customer steps involved. Then list:

- systems and providers that must integrate;
- migration, access-control, backup, monitoring, and support needs;
- the people responsible for daily operation and change approval;
- the tests, audit evidence, and regulatory requirements your institution expects.

The initial core focuses on internal, closed-loop activity. Check payment integrations, operational controls, testing, and regulatory evidence against your institution's requirements.

## For a developer

Map how a customer request reaches a staff review, reference calculation, and ledger entry. Source code and setup guides will be linked when available; use verified project documentation before building against implementation details.

## Understand the cost boundary

Open software has no licence fee. Hosting, migration, third-party services, implementation, support, and optional commercial modules can have separate costs. Confirm exact services, responsibilities, and pricing with the provider.
