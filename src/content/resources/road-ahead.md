This guide separates Higala's intended open scope from optional services and institution-specific work that needs further definition.

## Intended open scope

The open release is intended to include a double-entry ledger for internal activity, a staff workspace, a usable open reference loan engine, and a customer starter.

## Separate extensions and services

Talino's commercial loan engine is an optional extension. The intended open release does not require it. Talino's implementation, hosting, and support services are optional and can have separate costs.

## Work that needs its own evaluation

The initial core focuses on internal, closed-loop activity. External payment rails and provider integrations need separate decisions; institutions will need to plan migration, operations, access controls, monitoring, backups, and regulatory evidence for their own setting.
